# Help request

Review a support conversation, reply to the requester and maintain its routing and status. Concurrent changes may require a refresh before saving.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**Conversation / Load earlier messages**

Read messages with local display times. Load earlier messages retrieves older parts of the conversation.

**View screenshot**

Loads an image attached by the requester.

**Write a reply to the requester**

Enter the response. Replies are visible to the requester.

**Send reply**

Sends the message. The adjacent menu can send and change status together. A later requester reply reopens a closed ticket.

**Status**

Maintain New, In progress, Waiting on user or Closed as appropriate.

**Category / Queue / Assigned to**

Set the classification, destination queue and staff assignment permitted by your ticket scope.

**Assign to me**

Assigns the ticket to your application account.

**Save changes**

Saves routing and status changes. Retrying an unchanged submission does not create a duplicate reply.

**View device**

Opens the associated device when you have device access. Tickets retain their original device details if the device is removed.

## Permissions

Help Desk access and ticket-management scope are required. Device access uses your separate device permissions.
