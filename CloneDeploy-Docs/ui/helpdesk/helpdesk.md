# Help Desk

Find and manage support requests within your Help Desk access. Ticket visibility and management follow your assigned ticket and queue permissions.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**Search tickets**

Find requests by subject, requester or device.

**Queue / Category / Status**

Narrow the request list using the available queue, category and status filters. Clear a filter to broaden the results.

**Subject**

Select a request subject to open its conversation and routing details.

**Requester / Device**

Identify who submitted the request and the associated device. Device access is checked separately.

**Assigned to**

The staff account currently assigned to the request.

**Updated**

The most recent update time, displayed in local time.

**Previous / Next**

Browse additional matching tickets.

## Permissions

Help Desk access is required. Your grants determine whether you can manage your assignments, your queues, Unassigned or all tickets. These grants do not provide device or remote-control permissions.
