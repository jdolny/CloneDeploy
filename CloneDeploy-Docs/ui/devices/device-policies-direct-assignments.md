# Direct assignments

Maintain policies assigned directly to this device. Removing a direct assignment does not deactivate the policy or remove assignments inherited from groups or OUs.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**Edit assigned policies**

Opens the policy selection dialog.

**Search**

Filter the assigned-policy list, or find policies in the selection dialog.

**Policy selection**

Add the desired policies and remove unwanted direct assignments.

**Save Policies**

Saves the direct policy list. Cancel closes the dialog without applying edits.

## Permissions

Device View is required to open the device. Policy Edit is required to change direct assignments.
