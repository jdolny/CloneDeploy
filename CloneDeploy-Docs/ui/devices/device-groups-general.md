# General

Maintain the group identity, membership mode and tags. These settings determine how the group is organized and how its members are selected.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**Name**

The group name. The built-in All Devices group cannot be renamed.

**Description**

An administrative description of the group and its purpose.

**Membership mode**

Choose Manual for an explicit device list or Dynamic for saved matching criteria. The All Devices group has a fixed automatic membership mode.

**Add tag**

Choose a shared tag. Remove a chip to remove that tag from the pending selection.

**Save changes**

Saves the identifying fields, membership mode and tags. Configure the corresponding member list or criteria on Group Members.

## Permissions

Device Group View is required to open the group. Device Group Edit is required to save changes. Tag View permits loading available tags.
