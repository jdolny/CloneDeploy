# Effective Policy

Review active policies currently applicable to this device and where their assignments originate. Archived devices do not receive policies; login policies also depend on the user.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**Trigger**

Choose All triggers, Check-in, Startup or Login to narrow the policy list.

**Policy**

Select a policy name to open its targeting configuration.

**Assignment**

Shows the direct, group or directory source through which the policy applies.

**Download JSON**

Downloads the resolved configuration for diagnosis, including all matching policies for the selected trigger across every page. Keep it with other administrative information; it may contain deployment configuration.


**Copy JSON / Close (JSON viewer)**

In the resolved-policy viewer, Copy JSON copies the displayed configuration to the clipboard. Close dismisses the viewer without modifying or applying a policy. Browser clipboard permissions can prevent copying.

## Permissions

Device View is required to open the device.
