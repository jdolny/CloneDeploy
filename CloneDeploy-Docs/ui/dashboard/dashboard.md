# Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick overview of your managed devices, recent deployment activity, and service health. Use it to spot issues and decide what to investigate next. Select a count, chart, or notice to open the relevant records. Check the refreshed timestamp when interpreting the information.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Needs Attention** highlights items that may require follow-up, such as pending enrollment requests, failed tasks, updates awaiting approval, or server health issues. Select a notice to investigate. The section appears only when there are notices available to you; its absence does not mean the entire environment is free of issues.

Your Dashboard reflects your permissions. Different users may see different cards, notices, counts, and task results. Some users see only their own tasks, while users with broader access can see tasks across the environment. Administrators also see server-specific notices, including available CloneDeploy and main-server .NET updates.
