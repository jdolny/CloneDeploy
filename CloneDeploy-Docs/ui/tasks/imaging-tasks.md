# Imaging tasks

Monitor queued and running imaging work and inspect retained deployment outcomes.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**My tasks / All tasks**

Select your own work or all work available under your permissions.

**Active**

Inspect current capture, deployment and setup progress.

**Multicast**

Manage shared WIM transfer sessions and their participants.

**History**

Review retained results and recorded failures.

## Permissions

Imaging-task visibility and operations follow your assigned permissions.
