# Action deployments

Track direct action deployments and their device outcomes. Policies have separate execution history.

<div class="ui-controls-spacer"></div>

**My tasks / All tasks**

Choose your own work or all work available under your permissions.

**Active**

Review queued and running deployments and open one to inspect its device tasks.

**History**

Review completed and cancelled deployments. A deployment can contain different device outcomes.

## Permissions

Task visibility and management follow your assigned task permissions.
